CookSync

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Overview

CookSync is developed by Sean Howarth. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics or tracking.

Local storage

Your saved recipes, cooking plans, preferences and cooking progress are stored locally on your device. CookSync does not upload this information to a developer-operated server.

Recipe processing

Standard recipe processing takes place locally on your device. On compatible devices, enhanced recipe extraction uses Apple's on-device language model.

Website imports and recipe images

When you import a recipe from a website or view externally hosted recipe images, CookSync may connect directly to third-party websites and image hosts. These services may receive your IP address and other standard network request information. Their own privacy policies apply.

Sharing and clipboard access

CookSync processes recipe text and website addresses that you explicitly paste or share with the app. Shared recipes may be temporarily stored in a local container accessible to CookSync and its share extension.

Data collection

The developer does not collect personal information, recipe content, usage analytics or advertising identifiers through the CookSync app.

Deleting your data

You can delete saved recipes and end active cooking sessions within the app. Uninstalling CookSync removes its local app data, subject to operating system backup and restoration settings.

Support

Information you voluntarily submit through GitHub support is subject to GitHub's privacy policy. Public support requests may be visible to other people.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CookSync's functionality or privacy practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the CookSync support repository.
