CookSync

Cook together. Finish together.

Plan your meals, organise cooking tasks and bring everything together at your chosen serving time.

Getting started

Import a recipe from a supported website, paste recipe text or create your own meal plan. Review the cooking tasks, choose your serving time and follow your personalised cooking schedule.

Recipe importing

CookSync supports standard recipe processing without Apple Intelligence. Enhanced on-device extraction is available on compatible devices.

Imported recipes and cooking schedules may contain estimates. Always review cooking times, temperatures, ingredients and task order before cooking.
